Our Favorite Grooves

I'll have to Say I Love You in a Song by Jim Croce

Jim Croce picks a light, upbeat melody to pair with his heartfelt, "I love you" message. The melody and harmonies are layered to create a unique tone that is exclusive to Croce and his music. The guitar plays a strong starting role before fading into the rest of the instruments.

Pizza Angel: A Silly VeggieTales Song

While indeed a silly song about lost pizza, it explores the complexities of what memories and emotions this cuisine serves for the general population. Exploring the feelings of hangriness when it comes to waiting for food, this song connects us all through this shared experience.

Favorite Crime by Olivia Rodrigo

Olivia Rodrigo · favorite crime

Favorite Crime by Olivia Rodrigo is a simple picking melody accompanies the singers voice to keep listeners attention on the lyrics. Omitting percussives keep the song heartfelt and emotional, and the accompanying guitar pulls us in and keeps us listening.
